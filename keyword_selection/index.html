Rank Keyword Strategy

Strategy labels recommend keywords based on performance and placement trends. Keywords may be tagged with one or more of the following:

  • Battleground: Appears in middle item indexes organically
  • Complementary: Related to products that are commonly purchased or used alongside your product
  • Competitive: Associated with competing brands or products that shoppers may consider as alternatives to your product
  • Defend: Appears in the top item indexes organically
  • Drives Clicks: Yielded clicks in the lookback window
  • High Traffic: Top percentile of impressions in the lookback window
  • Potential: Appears in the bottom item indexes organically
  • Previously Boosted: Has been boosted in a past or current campaign
